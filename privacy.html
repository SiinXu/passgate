Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What PassGate collects, why, and who else sees it. Short version: only what we need to answer you and deliver what you buy. We never sell personal data.

Who we are

PassGate (passgate.incidental.site) is a product of Incidental AI. Incidental AI decides how the data described here is used.

What we collect

  • Interest form: your email, the plan and stack you pick, and your language.
  • Newsletter box: your email.
  • Purchases: Creem tells us your email, the name you entered, the plan, the amount and an order reference. We never see your card details.
  • Install service: the repository access and project details you choose to share for the job.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, and our own first-party event counter at stats.incidental.site. It records page views, clicks, scroll depth and form events, without your name or email. We use it only to see which pages work.

Your browser's local storage keeps your language and theme choice. There are no advertising trackers or advertising cookies on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, and to tell you when checkout opens if you asked.
  • To deliver your order, provide support and handle refunds.
  • To send the newsletter, only if you subscribed. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.
  • To protect the forms from spam and abuse.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the services that run PassGate: Creem (Armitage Labs OÜ), our merchant of record, for payments, tax and receipts; Cloudflare for hosting, data storage and email routing; and our email delivery provider for the messages we send. Creem keeps its own records as tax law requires, under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Interest and newsletter entries are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Order records are kept as long as accounting and tax rules require.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Email us from the address you used and we will act on it within 30 days. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this page, your data or an order? Email us and we reply within two business days.
